About FringeNode

FringeNode is AI engineering for living systems.

The aim

The land gets a brain and hands of its own, run by the people who live there.

The focus is artificial intelligence (AI) and robotics to restore land and wetlands. That means small solar-powered computers that learn from healthy wild land, and simple machines that help repair damaged land, with people kept at the center. The plan is written backward from that end goal — a method called backcasting — so each step is chosen because it leads there.

This site is a public record of that work: field guides, project notes, and later, hardware builds.

Field Guides

Five guides are out:

  • Field Guide 01, Understanding AI — a beginner guide to open-weight models, running them locally, agents, and privacy.
  • Field Guide 02, The AI Jargon Field Guide — the 19 terms to learn first for AI engineering and agent orchestration, plus a short glossary of 78 more.
  • Field Guide 03, Context Engineering in Practice — pack a focused window for one real job, and see why less often wins.
  • Field Guide 04, Your First Agent, Safely — design the safety in first, then build a small agent that can read and draft but cannot send, delete, or spend.
  • Field Guide 05, Evals for Beginners — write a small, honest test set for one job and reuse it after every change.
